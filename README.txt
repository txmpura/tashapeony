Peony
Open index.html in any modern browser. No install, no internet needed.

Drag to turn it. Space pauses, R starts over, 1 2 3 set the speed (1x, 4x, 12x),
S saves a PNG. Click or drag along the line at the bottom to jump in time.

Things to change (peony.js, top): LOOP total length, B0 when the bloom starts,
BT how many seconds it takes to open. Colours: the rgb list near the top.
